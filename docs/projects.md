# Creating and Opening Projects

Choose **Create Your Panorama** on the welcome screen and select your source images to create a project, or choose **Open Panorama…** to open an existing `.pw` project. Save your work with **File → Save** (Command-S).

A `.pw` project keeps your editing state so you can return to your work. Your source photographs remain external; saving the project does not copy them into it. Keep the original files available in their original locations.

If a project needs permission to read its photographs, follow [File Access / Grant Access](file-access.html).
